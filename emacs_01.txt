Using the Interactive Customization Menu (No Code)
This is the easiest method if you prefer a graphical menu interface.
Press M-x (usually Alt + x), type customize-variable,
"which does not work for me" and press Enter.
Type shift-select-mode and press Enter.
Click the [Toggle] button on the screen to switch between On (t) and Off (nil).
Click [Apply and Save] at the top of the buffer to store the setting permanently.
